2. Online Food Ordering Application

Scenario: A customer orders food through an online application.

Functions involved:
1. Display Menu – Show available food items.
2. Select Food – Choose food items.
3. Add to Cart – Add selected items to cart.
4. Remove Item – Remove unwanted food items.
5. Calculate Bill – Calculate total food cost.
6. Apply Coupon – Apply discount offers.
7. Make Payment – Complete online payment.
8. Track Order – Track food delivery status.
9. Cancel Order – Cancel the order.
=============================================
3. Online Movie Ticket Booking Application

Scenario: A customer wants to book movie tickets online.

Functions involved:
1. Display Movies – Show currently available movies.
2. Select Movie – Choose a movie.
3. Select Theatre – Choose a cinema theatre.
4. Select Date – Choose movie date.
5. Select Show Time – Select morning, afternoon or evening.
6. Check Seat Availability – Display available seats.
7. Book Tickets – Confirm selected seats.
8. Calculate Ticket Price – Calculate total ticket cost.
9. Make Payment – Complete payment.
10. Generate Ticket – Generate booking confirmation.

4. E-Commerce Shopping Application

Scenario: A customer purchases products through an online shopping website.

Functions involved:
1. User Registration – Create a new account.
2. User Login – Authenticate customer.
3. Display Products – Show available products.
4. Search Product – Search for a particular product.
5. Add to Cart – Add products to shopping cart.
6. Calculate Price – Calculate total amount.
7. Apply Discount – Apply promotional offers.
8. Calculate GST – Calculate applicable tax.
9. Payment – Process payment.
10. Order Tracking – Track delivery status.
